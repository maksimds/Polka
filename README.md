# Polka

Polka turns the notch at the top of your MacBook screen into a little shelf. Put files, folders, links, pictures and text on it, then grab them again whenever you need them. Just move your pointer to the notch and the shelf opens.

## What you need

- A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.
- A MacBook with a notch is best, but any Mac works. Screens without a notch get a small dark bar at the top middle instead.

## Download and open

1. Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/maksimds/Polka/releases) and download the latest **Polka.zip**.
2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click **Polka.zip** to unzip it.
3. Drag **Polka** into your **Applications** folder.
4. Double-click **Polka** in Applications to open it.
5. If your Mac says it can't check the app, click **Done**. Then open **System Settings**, go to **Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

Polka now shows a tray icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen, and an icon in the Dock.

## How to use it

### Open the shelf
Move your pointer to the notch. The shelf opens after a moment. Move away and it closes.

### Add things
Drag a file, folder, link, picture or some text up to the notch and let go. A pair of eyes shows you the shelf is ready to catch it.

You can also click **+** at the top of the shelf:
- **Link** to type a web address.
- **File** to pick files from your Mac.
- **Paste** to add whatever you last copied.

### Take things out
Drag an item from the shelf into any app, folder or email. It stays on the shelf, so you can use it again. Your original file is never moved.

### Open and preview
Double-click an item to open it. To peek without opening it, right-click and choose **Quick Look**.

### Share
Right-click an item and choose **Share** to send it with AirDrop, Mail, Messages, Notes and more, just like sharing from Finder. Links are shared as their web address, and text as text.

### Select several items
Click an item to select it. Hold **Command (⌘)** and click to add more, or drag a box around them. Then you can drag, preview, share or remove them all at once.

### Arrange and sort
Drag items around to put them in your own order, like apps on an iPhone. Or click the arrows button at the top of the shelf to sort by name, kind, file type or date added. Choose **None** to go back to arranging them yourself.

### Pin
Right-click an item and choose **Pin** to keep it in the top row, marked with a small red pin. Pinned items stay at the top however the shelf is sorted. You can pin up to five. To let one go, right-click it and choose **Unpin**.

### Rename
Right-click an item, choose **Rename**, type a new name and press **Return**. This only changes the name on the shelf. Your file keeps its real name.

### Remove
Point at an item and click the **×** on its corner, or right-click and choose **Remove**. This only takes it off the shelf. Your file stays where it is.

### Temporary items
Right-click an item and choose **Temporary**. It removes itself after 24 hours. You can pick a different time in Settings.

### Keep a copy
Polka remembers where your files are and follows them if you move or rename them. If you delete a file, its item shows **Unavailable**. To keep an item safe, right-click it and choose **Keep a Copy**. Polka then saves its own copy.

### Convert to PDF
Right-click a document (Word, RTF or plain text) and choose **Convert to PDF**. The PDF is saved next to the original and added to the shelf. Your Mac may ask once if Polka can save to that folder. Click **Allow**.

### Screenshots
In Settings, turn on **Save Screenshots to Polka**. New screenshots and screen recordings then go straight onto the shelf, a few seconds after you take them. They're temporary, and when they're removed they go to the Trash.

### Change the look
In Settings, under **Appearance**:
- **Transparency** lets your desktop show through the open shelf, softly blurred, like the sidebar in Finder. Slide it all the way to the left for the solid black shelf.
- **Color** tints the shelf. Click one of the swatches, or type a color code such as **#2881DB** in the **Hex** box and press **Return**. Polka turns your color into a deep, glassy gradient, so the names on the shelf always stay easy to read. Light colors like yellow or white come out darker. **Black** keeps the classic look.

If **Reduce transparency** is on in your Mac's Accessibility settings, the shelf stays solid.

### Settings
Click the tray icon in the menu bar and choose **Settings**, or click the gear at the top right of the shelf. Here you can change how the shelf looks, how quickly it opens, how close to the notch your pointer needs to be, and how long temporary items last.

### Start Polka automatically
Click the tray icon in the menu bar and choose **Open at Login**. Polka will then start every time you log in to your Mac.

### More than one screen
The shelf follows your pointer to whichever screen you're using.

### Quit
Click the tray icon in the menu bar and choose **Quit**.
