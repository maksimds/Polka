# Polka

Polka turns the notch at the top of your MacBook screen into a little shelf. Put files, folders, links, pictures and text on it, sort them into folders, then grab them again whenever you need them. You can even send them to a Mac nearby. Just move your pointer to the notch and the shelf opens.

## What you need

- A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.
- A MacBook with a notch is best, but any Mac works. Screens without a notch get a small dark bar at the top middle instead.
- To send things to another Mac, Polka on both Macs and Wi-Fi turned on. They don't need to be on the same network.

## Download and open

1. Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/maksimds/Polka/releases) and download the latest **Polka.zip**.
2. Open your Downloads folder and double-click **Polka.zip** to unzip it.
3. Drag **Polka** into your **Applications** folder.
4. Double-click **Polka** in Applications to open it.
5. If your Mac says it can't check the app, click **Done**. Then open **System Settings**, go to **Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

Polka now shows a tray icon in the menu bar at the top of your screen, and an icon in the Dock.

## How to use it

### Open the shelf
Move your pointer to the notch. The shelf opens after a moment. Move away and it closes.

### Add things
Drag a file, folder, link, picture or some text up to the notch and let go. A pair of eyes shows you the shelf is ready to catch it.

You can also click **+** at the top of the shelf:
- **Link** to type a web address.
- **File** to pick files from your Mac.
- **Folder** to make a folder on the shelf (see Folders below).
- **Paste** to add whatever you last copied.

Or point at the open shelf and press **Command-V (⌘V)** to paste. Right-clicking an empty spot on the shelf also gives you **New Folder** and **Paste**.

If something is already on the shelf, Polka tells you instead of adding it twice.

### Take things out
Drag an item from the shelf into any app, folder or email. It stays on the shelf, so you can use it again. Your original file is never moved.

To copy instead, right-click an item and choose **Copy**, or select items and press **Command-C (⌘C)** while your pointer is over the shelf. Then paste wherever you like: Finder gets a copy of the file, and an email gets it attached.

### Open and preview
Double-click an item to open it. To peek without opening it, right-click and choose **Quick Look**. **Get Info** shows the same info window as Finder.

### Share
Right-click an item and choose **Share** to send it with AirDrop, Mail, Messages, Notes and more, just like sharing from Finder. Links are shared as their web address, and text as text.

### Select several items
Click an item to select it. Hold **Command (⌘)** and click to add more, hold **Shift (⇧)** and click to select everything in between, or drag a box around them. Then you can drag, copy, preview, share, send or remove them all at once.

### Arrange and sort
Drag items around to put them in your own order, like apps on an iPhone. Or click the arrows button at the top of the shelf to sort by name, kind, file type or date added. Choose **None** to go back to arranging them yourself.

### Folders
Folders help you keep the shelf tidy. Click **+** and choose **Folder**, or right-click an empty spot on the shelf and choose **New Folder**. Type a name and press **Return**.

To put items in a folder, drag them onto it, or right-click and choose **Move To**. Double-click a folder to open it. Click **Polka** at the top left to go back to the shelf, or drag items onto **Polka** to move them back out. Anything you add while a folder is open goes into it.

When you drag something from another app up to the notch, your folders show up under the drop area, so you can drop it straight into one.

Each folder has its own sort order and its own pinned items. Folders only live on Polka's shelf: nothing is created or moved in Finder, and your files stay where they are. A folder can't be dragged out of Polka, but you can right-click it and choose **Compress** to get everything in it as one .zip file, added to the shelf next to the folder.

### Pin
Right-click an item and choose **Pin** to keep it in the top row, marked with a small red pin. Pinned items stay at the top however the shelf is sorted. You can pin up to five on the shelf, and five in each folder. To let one go, right-click it and choose **Unpin**.

### Rename
Right-click an item and choose **Rename**:
- **Rename Label** changes the name on the shelf only. Your file keeps its real name.
- **Rename File** renames the file itself, on your Mac (**Rename Folder** for a folder from Finder). The ending, like .pdf, can't be changed.
- **Edit Address** changes a link's web address.

Type the new name and press **Return**, or press **Esc** to keep the old one. Text and Polka's own folders just have **Rename**.

### Remove
Point at an item and click the **×** on its corner, or right-click and choose **Remove**. This only takes it off the shelf. Your file stays where it is. Removing a folder puts the items inside it back on the shelf.

### Keep a copy
Polka remembers where your files are and follows them if you move or rename them. If you delete a file, its item shows **Unavailable**. To keep an item safe, right-click it and choose **Keep a Copy**. Polka then saves its own copy.

### Convert to PDF
Right-click a document (Word, RTF or plain text) and choose **Convert to PDF**. The PDF is saved next to the original and added to the shelf. Your Mac may ask once if Polka can save to that folder. Click **Allow**.

### Screenshots
In Settings, turn on **Save Screenshots to Polka**. New screenshots and screen recordings then go straight onto the shelf, a few seconds after you take them. When you remove one, its file goes to the Trash.

### Send to a Mac nearby
Polka can send items straight to another Mac's shelf, without AirDrop.

**Turn it on.** Open Settings, click the **Nearby Sharing** tab and turn on **Share with Macs Nearby**. Your Mac asks once if Polka can find devices on your local network. Click **Allow**. Under **Name** you can change the name other Macs see.

**Send.** Right-click an item, choose **Send To** and pick a Mac. Or start dragging an item on the shelf, or a file, link or text from another app up to the notch: a small box with the Macs nearby appears beside the shelf, and you drop it on the one you want. You can send files, links and text, up to 50 items and 1 GB at a time. Folders can't be sent.

**Receive.** When someone sends you something, the shelf opens and shows what it is. Click **Accept** to put it on your shelf, or **Decline** to say no. **Decline and Block** also turns that Mac away, and for the next 10 minutes stops offers from any Mac you haven't remembered.

**Remember a Mac you trust.** On both Macs, open the **Nearby Sharing** tab and click **Remember a Nearby Mac…**, then pick the other Mac on one of them. Both show a 6-digit code. If the codes are the same, click **Codes Match** on both. From then on, items between you arrive without asking. To stay hidden from everyone else, set **Receive From** to **Remembered Macs Only**. You can **Forget** a Mac at any time.

### Change the look
In Settings, under **Appearance**:
- **Transparency** lets your desktop show through the open shelf, softly blurred, like the sidebar in Finder. Slide it all the way to the left for the solid black shelf.
- **Color** tints the shelf. Pick a shade on the rainbow bar, then drag in the square above it, or type a color code such as **#2881DB** in the **Hex** box and press **Return**. Polka turns your color into a deep, glassy gradient, so the names on the shelf always stay easy to read. The block on the left shows how the shelf will look, so light colors like yellow or white come out darker there. Drag to the very bottom of the square for the classic black.

If **Reduce transparency** is on in your Mac's Accessibility settings, the shelf stays solid.

### Settings
Click the tray icon in the menu bar and choose **Settings**, or click the gear at the top right of the shelf. The **General** tab is where you change how the shelf looks, how quickly it opens, how close to the notch your pointer needs to be, and where screenshots go. The **Nearby Sharing** tab is for sending to other Macs.

### Start Polka automatically
Click the tray icon in the menu bar and choose **Open at Login**, or turn it on in Settings. Polka will then start every time you log in to your Mac.

### More than one screen
The shelf follows your pointer to whichever screen you're using.

### Quit
Click the tray icon in the menu bar and choose **Quit**.
